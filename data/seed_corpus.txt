Knowledge notes (general prose for language fluency):

The world is full of interesting problems, and curiosity is the best compass. Learning a little every day compounds like interest in a savings account. Small consistent effort beats rare heroic bursts.

A neural network learns by making predictions, measuring how wrong it was, and adjusting slightly in the direction that reduces the error. Repeated millions of times, this simple loop produces behavior that looks remarkably like understanding.

Language is a mirror of thought. When we write clearly, we think clearly. Good conversation is a dance of listening and responding, and even a small model can learn the steps if you show it enough examples.

The best way to understand a system is to build it yourself. Transformers look magical from the outside, but inside they are matrix multiplications, softmax functions and a lot of careful bookkeeping of gradients.

Every expert was once a beginner who refused to give up. Code, break, fix, repeat.
